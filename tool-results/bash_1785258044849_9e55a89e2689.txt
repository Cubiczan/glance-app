Hey Hacker, and welcome!
WTF!! for Write The Future, of course 😁
iExec is launching the WTF Hackathon Summer Edition, an online builder challenge to create use cases built on Nox, the confidential smart contract layer of iExec.
We previously ran the VIBE Coding Hackathon, where builders shipped some amazing DeFi and RWA projects, Payments &amp; Payroll, DeFi &amp; Lending, Vaults &amp; Yields, OTC &amp; Trading, Prediction Markets &amp; Auctions, RWA &amp; Real Estate, Fundraising / VC, and Invoicing.
And that was cool, ⚠️⚠️
!! ⚠️⚠️
The Challenge
We're looking for builders who can take a real, impactful open-source protocol and add privacy or build/merge a project truly innovative with Nox .
Don't just build a proof-of-concept. We want to see a protocol integration clean enough that it could become a real product with. By integrating Nox into existing open-source infrastructure, you're enhancing it with privacy at the core.
Here's the catch: these protocols aren't built for privacy, they're public by design. Build on top of them using Nox to maintain privacy while interacting with transparent infrastructure through batching, layering or other mechanisms.
Maintain the privacy guarantee without needing to modify the underlying protocols. Route a swap through Nox without breaking composability, that's where the real value lies.
Suggested open-source targets:
⚠️⚠️ You're not required to pick any of these ideas feel free to innovate!
All Wallets like MetaMask, Rabby, Rainbow: add privacy flows to an existing wallet
DeFi Aave, Uniswap, Curve: route swaps or lending through Nox confidential contracts
Treasury Safe, Sablier, Superfluid: enable private payouts, streams or treasury moves
Judged on how cleanly Nox integrates into the app, the privacy it adds, and how close the result is to something a company can deploy.
Any builder reusing a project from the previous Vibe Coding Hackathon will be disqualified (you can validate your project idea with us anytime)
What is Nox - Protocol Layer
Nox is a confidential computing layer that enables computations on encrypted data while preserving full DeFi composability. It combines on-chain smart contracts with off-chain Trusted Execution Environments (TEE) to process encrypted data without ever exposing plaintext on-chain.
With Nox, you can build confidential smart contracts Your contracts can process encrypted inputs, execute computations privately, and maintain hidden balances, without asking users to change wallets or developers to rewrite contracts.
